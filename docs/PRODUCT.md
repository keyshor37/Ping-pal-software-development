# Product statement · PingPal

## Client
Recreational racket-sport players (students and young adults) in the Helsinki region who want regular playing partners. Personas are anonymised.

## The process today
Players ask around in group chats or social media, wait for replies, and often cannot find someone of a similar skill level nearby.

## Baseline metric
Time to find a suitable playing partner (minutes per search). To be measured with interviewees.

## The statement
When a player wants a game but doesn't know anyone at their level nearby, PingPal lets them browse and filter players by city, sport and skill level, so that the time to find a partner goes down.

## Out of scope
- Coaches and coaching profiles
- Messaging, bookings and court reservations
- Payments and donations
